Quick couple of updates for tomorrow night’s Friendsgiving Celebration! We’ve had to do a bit of a lineup swap but have no fear we still have some great DJs throwing down all night plus Open Decks is still on offer from 7pm to 9pm! Pot Luck is welcome and encouraged for those of you wishing to bring food to share! We are really hoping to have a successful canned food drive to give to people in need for this Thanksgiving! On Saturday November 16th Subculture is hosting another FREE community event with absolutely zero cover! We’ll be accepting canned food donations to help out families in need for this Thanksgiving so participation is highly encouraged! The night will start off with Open Decks from 7pm to 9pm followed by sets from @creighfish_bass @shawnzywonzie @t1lt3d_ @realcoletrickle and @officialparkit and maybe even some additional special guests! As always we will have live artists slinging colors all night and some special deals on original art pieces! This one’s for the community!
