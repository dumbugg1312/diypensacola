submit a show

booking a diy or punk show in pensacola? send us the flyer and we'll put it on the calendar, free, no gatekeeping.

submit your show (google form) ↗

or message us:

dm on instagram ↗ post in discord ↗

here's what to include:

  • the date
  • the venue (house or diy space? see the note below about the address)
  • set time / doors
  • cover: free, sliding scale, or a $ amount
  • all ages, 18+, or 21+
  • who's playing (the full lineup)
  • the flyer image
  • is it a benefit? who or what it's for

house show? we never publish the address. people see “dm for the address” and ask you. send it by dm or the form, never in discord.

what we list: punk, metal, hardcore, diy and lgbtq shows. a corporate night or a cover-band night usually won't go up.

no flyer yet? just send the details, we'll make it work. shows go up by hand, usually within a day.

changes and corrections

need to change or cancel a listing? same places. wrong date, a missing act, a name spelled wrong, a show that moved: send it the same ways and copy the link of the page that has it wrong.

who this is

diypensacola is a free community project for the scene. no ads, no ticket fees, nothing to sign up for. flyer info comes straight from the venues and the people booking, and listings go up by hand. to reach a person, dm @diypensacola on instagram or message a mod in the discord. if it's useful to you, the donate link in the footer is how you chip in.
